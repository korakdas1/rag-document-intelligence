# Evaluation Protocol

Synthetic protocol for a retrieval study inside this repository.

## Human ratings

Human raters scored faithfulness on a three-point scale: unsupported, partial, fully supported.
Raters saw the answer and the evidence bundle, not the full corpus.

## Automatic retrieval metrics

Retrieval was measured with Recall@10 and MRR on a held-out question set.
Do not collapse retrieval and generation into a single accuracy number.
