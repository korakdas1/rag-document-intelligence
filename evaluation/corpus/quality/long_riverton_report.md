# Cedar Field Station operations report (synthetic long slice)

This file is longer than the other qualitybench notes so early, middle, and late facts sit in different sections. It is still a small synthetic document, not a scanned paper.

## Early history

Cedar Field Station was founded in 2004 at Harbor Mill.
The founding charter limited the station to regional transit measurement. It did not authorize ferry operations.
The first badge printers arrived in 2005.

The early years used clipboard counts. Those clipboard sheets were discarded in 2010 and are not in this archive.

## Weather and demand (middle)

The 2016 ice storm reduced weekday boardings by 31 percent for two weeks.
The reduction is attributed to road closures north of Riverton, not to a fare change.
No weekend census exists for that storm window.

Maintenance crews sanded Platform 1 and Platform 2. Platform 3 stayed closed for six days.

## Maintenance

Page 7 records that brake inspections occur every 8,000 km.
Wiper blades are replaced every 18 months.
Tunnel radios are tested every Monday at 04:30.

## Late upgrades

The tunnel GPS retrofit was completed in November 2023.
After the retrofit, river-tunnel tracks no longer drop for more than 40 seconds.
The retrofit did not add cellular coverage on the coastal bluff.

## Findings

The badge-tap census remains the only published ridership method.
Harbor Mill remains the station site.
The report does not state a 2018 launch date; launch dates live in the overview and errata files.
