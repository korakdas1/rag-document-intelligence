# Riverton Transit Loop — overview

Internal briefing for the Cedar Field Station archive. This document is synthetic evaluation text.

## Launch

The Riverton Transit Loop (RTL) launched on 12 March 2018 in Riverton, Oregon.
The system identifier is RTL-2018-A.
Mira Solano is the operations director.

## Fleet and demand

The revenue fleet contains 42 vehicles.
Weekday ridership is 18,400 boardings.
The loop does not serve the coastal ferry terminal.

## Distractor geography

Harbor Mill is inland. Mentions of the coastal bluff in other files do not mean the loop runs there.

## Definition

In this archive, a “boarding” means a badge-tap at a platform validator, not a paper ticket scan.
