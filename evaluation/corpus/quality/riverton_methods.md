# Riverton Transit Loop — methods

## Census method

Ridership was counted with a badge-tap census, not passenger surveys.
The sampling window is weekdays 06:00–22:00.
School-only shuttles are excluded from the census.

## Comparison baseline

Infrared gate counts were rejected because they double-count transferring riders.
Dana Quill proposed the badge-tap method in the 2017 methods memo.
