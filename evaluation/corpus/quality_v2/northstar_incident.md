# Northstar incident memorandum: dropout at rack M2

Record NBP-IM, preliminary assessment, 4 June 2024. This short memorandum preserves the first interpretation of a monitoring interruption. It does not close the investigation or override a later explicit correction.

## Observation

The preliminary Northstar memorandum attributes the M2 dropout to a suspected temperature-sensor failure.
The operator saw a flat channel after reconnecting the rack. The display resumed after the plug was reseated, but reseating changed both the sensor connection and the harness connection. That intervention could not distinguish the two explanations. The affected trace was quarantined rather than smoothed into a continuous run.

## Immediate response

The technician isolated the rack, photographed the latch, and placed the probe and lead in separate evidence sleeves. The pilot continued only on unaffected holders. No estimate of repair cost was included in the engineering incident log. A final cause required continuity testing under movement as well as a stationary resistance check.

## Transfer planning attachment

For the 19 June Northstar transfer, the incident memorandum allocates 8 reserve modules to Glassmere Yard.
This refers to the same transfer lot and destination as the planning brief, not a second shipment. Neither record is designated authoritative for that allocation, and the discrepancy remains open. The attachment was copied from a logistics worksheet, not derived from the number of faulty components. Closing the electrical incident would not by itself settle the reserve inventory.

The incident owner requested that future summaries distinguish the preliminary diagnosis from the eventual finding. An explanation can be revised without deleting the observations that motivated it. The original trace and the removed harness remain part of the same investigation record.
