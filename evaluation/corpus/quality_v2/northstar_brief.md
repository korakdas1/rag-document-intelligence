# Northstar Battery Pilot: project brief

Document NBP-BR, issued 22 January 2024. This is an original synthetic engineering record. Names, organizations, measurements, and events belong to the fictional Northstar project.

## Purpose and boundary

The Northstar project identifier is NBP-24C.
The pilot examines stationary backup packs for a small instrument shelter at Glassmere Yard. It does not qualify a traction battery, a medical power supply, or an unattended domestic installation. A successful laboratory validation permits another engineering review; it is not a commercial product release. The shelter's monitoring equipment must continue to log safely when a pack is disconnected for inspection.

The project separates cell retention, pack standby load, and calibration load. These quantities share a test rack but answer different questions. A low standby current is useful only if the protection circuit still detects a disconnected lead. A high retained-capacity result does not establish the reliability of that detection path. Reviewers should keep the measured unit beside each result when copying values into the approval sheet.

## Responsibilities

Arin Cole, the sample custodian, reports to project lead Leena Voss rather than to the rack contractor.
The contractor controls access to the electrical enclosure but cannot release a held sample. The custodian reconciles incoming labels, checks seals after transport, and records any mismatch before a technician starts calibration. Leena signs the pilot decision after the custodian and safety reviewer have completed separate sections of the record. A signature for receipt is not a signature for experimental acceptance.

## Material planning

For the 19 June Northstar transfer, the planning brief allocates 6 reserve modules to Glassmere Yard.
The reserve allocation concerns the same sealed transfer lot discussed in the incident memorandum. Neither the planning brief nor that memorandum has priority for resolving an allocation discrepancy. The transfer ticket was left unsigned while the two owners checked their copies. This allocation is separate from the number of individual cells received for laboratory screening.

Packing sleeves carry both a serial label and a rack-position label. The position can change during randomization; the serial cannot. A replacement sleeve must reproduce the serial exactly and preserve the removed sleeve in the custody envelope. Reusing an empty slot does not authorize reusing the result from the previous occupant. The receiving clerk checks this distinction because two packs can have the same outward appearance.

## Reading the project record

The protocol records the proposed acceptance rule and operating sequence. The operations report records measurements, deviations, and archive handling. The incident memorandum is a preliminary diagnosis, while the later amendment explicitly closes the dropout investigation. A correction applies only to the fields named in that amendment. Unrelated disagreements must remain visible instead of being reconciled from the order of filenames.

Commercial terms are outside this brief. Validation staff collected operational observations, not a price schedule or an insurance decision. Any future purchasing pack would need its own evidence and owner. The project record should not be used to fill absent commercial fields by analogy with other battery products.
