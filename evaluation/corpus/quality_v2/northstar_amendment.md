# Northstar signed amendment: acceptance and incident closure

Record NBP-AM2. This notice is part of the fictional Northstar engineering archive, not a specification for a real battery.

## Effective decision

The Northstar acceptance amendment took effect on 19 June 2024.
The signature date is the effective date for new acceptance decisions. Earlier runs keep their acquisition dates and raw results. Reassessing a result under the new rule must produce a separate decision row rather than changing the recorded measurement. The sample custodian retains the previous decision so that another reviewer can reconstruct the sequence.

The final Northstar threshold is 78% retained capacity; this supersedes revision A's proposed 74% threshold.
The change applies to the acceptance gate, not to the discharge instrument or the definition of a cycle. Packs that passed a draft discussion gate are not automatically released under the final rule. A summary of the current threshold should lead with the final percentage and identify the earlier percentage only when a historical comparison is requested.

## Scope of authorization

Northstar is approved for attended pilot testing but is not approved for production deployment.
The authorization requires a trained observer with access to the isolation switch. It covers the documented shelter arrangement, not arbitrary downstream equipment. A successful pilot is evidence for a future review, not a promise that the future review will approve release. The production decision remains negative even though the pilot decision is positive.

## Root cause correction

The final Northstar investigation found a wiring-harness fault, superseding the preliminary sensor-failure diagnosis.
Movement at the latch opened a conductor whose stationary resistance was normal. The probe itself remained stable when connected through a reference lead. Replacing the harness removed the dropout under the documented movement check. The evidence supports this electrical explanation; it does not establish a general failure rate for all harnesses of that design.

The closed incident retains both the preliminary memorandum and the final continuity trace. Search results may therefore contain the abandoned hypothesis beside the correction. Readers should treat the explicit supersession as the authority signal, rather than choosing whichever statement has the strongest wording. A copied paragraph without its document title is insufficient for a release decision.

## Matters not resolved by this notice

This amendment does not reconcile the conflicting reserve-module allocations or the competing M2 inspection schedules.
Those records have separate owners and require their own signed reconciliation. This notice's later issue date must not be used to assign priority to an unrelated planning field. The archive likewise contains no warranty term, project budget, or measured cold-room capacity. Those omissions are intentional boundaries of the engineering record, not zero values.

When preparing a handover, attach the final gate and the appropriate observation. Do not replace an observed result with the acceptance threshold, and do not combine a quiet-load measurement with a calibration-fixture load. Each field should remain traceable to a named document and operating condition.
