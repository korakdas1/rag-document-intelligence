# Meridian field incident and coverage note

Record MTS-IN, 23 April 2024. Preliminary field memorandum, maintained separately from the operations report.

## Bracket incident

The preliminary Meridian incident note suspects clock drift as the cause of the April amplitude drop.
The display showed reduced pulse amplitude after a windy maintenance interval. Staff initially compared the instrument clock with the server clock, because earlier upload delays had looked like missing events. This hypothesis did not explain why the amplitude changed when the housing moved. The release notice records the final mechanical finding.

## Disputed event ledger

For the Meridian corridor C outage on 18 April, the incident ledger counts 19 invalid pings.
This is the same outage and validity definition used by the operations ledger, which gives another count. Neither ledger is the authoritative reconciliation. The raw files survived, but the joint recount had not been signed when this note closed. The discrepancy is not a difference between invalid pings and all messages.

## Coverage limits

Meridian's count can miss a badge edge when a stroller blocks the exit-line view.
The observer marks such occlusion rather than treating the absence of an instrument pulse as proof of no departure. These observations do not supply a wheelchair-specific detection rate. The limited event sheets also cannot recover the duration of a rider's journey before reaching the platform.

Meridian's departure dataset cannot infer rider income.
No income variable or linked survey was collected. Aggregate station activity should not be treated as a proxy that fills this absent field. The limitation concerns what the data contain, independently of how accurately the instrument detects a visible crossing.
